Privacy Policy for TapRush

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how TapRush ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

TapRush does not operate a server that collects your information directly. The app stores the initials you enter for high scores and your scores locally on your device. We also use third-party advertising services that may collect information as described below.

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display banner and rewarded advertisements. The rewarded ad is shown only when you choose to continue a game with one extra life. Depending on your consent choices and applicable law, Google may collect and use information to provide personalized or non-personalized ads. This may include:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Device information (model, operating system)
  • Ad interaction data

TapRush uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage consent where required, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland. You can change your consent choices through the privacy options provided by the app when available.

For more information about how Google uses data, please visit: Google Privacy Policy

Permissions

TapRush requests the following permissions:

  • Internet: Required to load and display advertisements
  • Network State: Used to check network connectivity for ad loading
  • Vibrate: Used for haptic feedback during gameplay

Data Storage

TapRush stores high scores and the initials entered for those scores in a local database. The app does not send this game data to a TapRush server. Android backup may include this local database and app preferences, depending on your device and backup settings.

Children's Privacy

TapRush is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: For banner and rewarded advertisements - Privacy Policy
  • Google User Messaging Platform: For managing advertising consent - Privacy Policy

Your Choices

You can change your advertising consent choices through the privacy options shown by the app when available. You can also opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings:

  • On Android: Go to Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
